Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

HelloMacroCalculator provides free, browser-based tools that estimate calorie and macronutrient targets. This policy explains what information is involved when you use this website.

Information you enter

Calculator values such as age, height, weight, activity level, and nutrition preferences are processed in your browser to produce your result. The calculator does not require an account. We do not intentionally transmit these values to our servers through the calculator code.

If you choose to use your browser’s share feature, the text you share is sent through the sharing destination you select. If you copy results or print/save a PDF, that action is handled by your browser and device.

Cookies and analytics

The current calculator pages do not set their own analytics or advertising cookies. The site may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can disclose technical request information such as your IP address to that provider. Browser settings or future hosting, analytics, or advertising integrations may affect information processing; this policy should be updated if those features are added.

Third-party services

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or data practices.

Children’s privacy

This general wellness tool is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not designed for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date. For privacy questions, contact the website operator through the contact method provided on the site.
